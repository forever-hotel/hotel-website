Locally served fonts
====================

Manrope is used for body copy, navigation, forms and buttons. Cormorant Garamond
is used for headings and the hotel wordmark. The variable Latin WOFF2 files are
served through `next/font/local`, with system fallbacks for other scripts.

Both fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License; their licence files
are included here. Sources: Google Fonts' Manrope and Cormorant Garamond families
(`https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope` and
`https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond`).

The browser does not contact a font provider. The two WOFF2 files total about
61 KB before HTTP compression.
